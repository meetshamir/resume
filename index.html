Shamir is a Principal Product Manager at Microsoft Core AI who designs and ships agentic AI products end-to-end — from concept to code to customer adoption. He helped define and drive the adoption of Azure SRE Agent, an end-to-end agentic platform for any kind of operational toil, and personally builds tools like Docify (end-to-end agentic documentation) and CaseAI (automated insights and recommendations to improve product supportability). His focus: building the agents that move enterprises toward ZeroOps — where production systems detect, diagnose, and resolve issues without humans in the loop.

CaseAI · Self-serve Agentic Insights

CaseAI: turn a backlog of support tickets into product insights — in minutes.

CaseAI is a self-serve agentic application that analyzes customer support tickets using OpenAI and generates actionable insights to drive product quality and supportability improvements. Zero onboarding cost, no configuration, no prompting required — instantly onboard any new product. Explore trends by support topic, root-cause paths, or AI-generated problem areas. Analyze up to 200 cases in under 5 minutes, and surface engineering escalations instantly.

→ 1M+ tickets analyzed across 150+ Microsoft products
